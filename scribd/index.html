SCRIBD · VERSION 1.0.1

Scribd PDF
Exporter.

Automatically scroll through pages available to your current account, capture the rendered page surface and assemble an image-based PDF locally.

Manual download available

Install the extension manually

Download and extract the package, then open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode and choose Load unpacked.

Download Scribd v1.0.1 ↓
01

Go to the reader

Open a Scribd document page, then select Go Download. KTools redirects to Scribd's scroll reader without adding an access key.

02

Choose the output

In the reader, hover over the PDF button and set the file name, capture resolution and JPEG compression.

03

Let the scan finish

Keep the Scribd reader tab open while KTools discovers rendered pages and creates the PDF.
