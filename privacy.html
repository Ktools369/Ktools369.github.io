Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the KTools Drive PDF Exporter Chrome extension and the KTools website.

Data collection

KTools does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information, document contents, browsing history or generated PDF files.

How the extension works

The extension operates on Google Drive pages opened by the user. It reads viewer metadata and page images required for the requested export, processes those images locally in the browser and creates a PDF on the user's device. This data is not sent to KTools servers.

Permissions and website access

The extension's access is limited to https://drive.google.com/* because its export interface and document processing operate inside the Drive viewer. The extension toolbar icon and in-product brand link can open https://ktools369.github.io/.

Storage

The extension does not use remote storage and does not maintain user accounts. The KTools website is a static GitHub Pages website. GitHub may process basic request information according to its own privacy documentation.

Third-party services

Google Drive and GitHub are independent services governed by their respective terms and privacy policies. KTools is not affiliated with Google or GitHub.

Children's privacy

KTools is not designed to collect information from children and knowingly collects no personal data.

Changes

Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
