How can we help?

For installation help, bug reports or privacy questions, email kie.dev369@gmail.com.

Include in a bug report

  • Chrome version and operating system
  • KTools extension version
  • Document type and approximate page count
  • The exact error message or a screenshot

Quick checks

  1. Confirm the extension is enabled at chrome://extensions.
  2. Reload the Google Drive viewer after installing or updating.
  3. Keep the Drive tab open until the export is complete.
  4. Clear old entries from the extension Errors page before reproducing an issue.

Please do not email private documents, passwords, session cookies or Google account credentials.
