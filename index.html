IMPORTANT

Use documents responsibly

Which documents can I export?

Only documents you own or have permission to save. KTools does not grant rights to content and should not be used to defeat access controls.

Does KTools receive my pages?

No. Page retrieval and PDF assembly happen locally in the browser. The website provides product information and links to the official Chrome Web Store listing.

Is the output searchable?

The generated PDF is image-based and does not contain an OCR text layer.
